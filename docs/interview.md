1. Open dashboard.
2. Add an expense and show automatic categorization.
3. Show budget health.
4. Open Engineering & product evidence.
5. Explain the 1,000+ TPS benchmark plan.
6. Explain the 20% categorization experiment.
7. Explain MTTR reduction and observability.
8. Explain the budget-visibility study.

Be explicit about which parts you personally implemented and which were collaborative.
